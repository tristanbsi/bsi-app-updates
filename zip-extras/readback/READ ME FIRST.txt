ReadBack — read me first

ReadBack turns comms channels into live text on a Mac, and shares them with phones and laptops on the network.
It needs macOS 26 (Tahoe) on an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

INSTALL
1. Drag ReadBack.app into your Applications folder.
2. The first time, right-click (or Control-click) ReadBack and choose Open, then Open again in the box that appears.
   macOS shows a warning because ReadBack comes from BSI, not the App Store. You only do this once.
   If it says the app "can't be opened", open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
3. It starts on a made-up test feed of show comms. To use real audio: Settings (⌘,) › Audio & channels, pick your
   interface (or Dante Virtual Soundcard, or the Mac's microphone), and say Allow when macOS asks about the microphone.

UPDATES
ReadBack checks for new versions by itself. When one is ready it tells you; ReadBack › Check for Updates… installs it.
You never need to download this zip again. If an update misbehaves, ReadBack › Go Back to Previous Version… undoes it.

HELP
Help › ReadBack Manual explains every screen.

TROUBLE
- "Can't be opened because Apple cannot check it": use the right-click › Open steps above.
- No text appears: check Settings › Audio & channels: the right source, each channel on, its meter moving.
- Still stuck: send Tristan a note (info@bsiproduction.com) with what you clicked and what you saw.
